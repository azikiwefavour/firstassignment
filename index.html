Beef Burger

A simple, flavorful burger perfect for grilling season!

Recipe Details

  • Prep Time
  • Cook Time
  • Servings

Ingredients

  • Ground beef
  • lettuce
  • onions
  • Egg
  • Bacon

Instructions

  1. Preheat your pan on medium-high and cut ground beef into golf ball sized
  2. Get smashing.Drop the meat in the pan,hold for 5 seconds
  3. Flip and cheese
  4. Ready the bun
  5. Assemble the smashed burger creation and dig in
